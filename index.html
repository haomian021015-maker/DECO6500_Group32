Task Instructions

You will be shown a series of food cards. Sort each card into one of the two areas below.

Understanding the scenario and “Today”

When you open a Scenario Card, you will see a date labelled “Today”. This is the simulated date on which you are assessing the food, and the day it is due to be removed from sale. Use this date together with the clues provided to decide whether the food can still be rescued or should be sent for non-food recycling.

What do the Food Rescue and Discard buttons do?

Food Rescue

Click Food Rescue to move the card to the Food Rescue area. Choose this option if you believe the food is still suitable for human consumption and can be donated to a food rescue organisation.

Discard — Non-food Recycling

Click Discard to move the card to the Disposal area. Choose this option if you believe the food is no longer suitable for human consumption and should be sent to an organisation that recycles it for non-food uses.

Timing and changing your decisions

  • You have 3 minutes to sort all of the cards.
  • You may change a decision at any time before you click End the Timing.
  • To change a card you have already sorted, click that card — it will return to the sorting flow.
  • When you have finished all of the cards, click End the Timing.

Additional notes for Phase B

  • In Phase B the system provides a suggested classification for every card.
  • If you agree with the suggestion, click Confirm.
  • If you think the suggestion is wrong, or you notice another problem, click Change and write your feedback.

After you submit feedback, the card is moved to the Disposal area, and the decision is recorded as a change you made based on your own judgement.

Selection history records every action in order, including the first classification, changed answers and repeated choices such as Food Rescue → Food Rescue. Card visits are listed in the order opened, with each visit timed separately in seconds. “Card 1 (2)” means the second visit to Card 1. Times include close-ups and feedback, but exclude time on the sorting screen. Older records show totals only; “—” means no visits were recorded or timing data is unavailable.
